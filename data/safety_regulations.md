# Site Safety Regulations and General Code of Conduct

## Section 1: Site Access and Authorization
All personnel, contractors, and site visitors must check in at the main safety gate before entering active construction zones. Access is strictly prohibited to anyone without valid induction training, an active site access badge, and mandatory Level 1 PPE. Operating vehicles must adhere to a strict site speed limit of 15 km/h (10 mph) on all temporary access roads.

## Section 2: Housekeeping and Walkway Clearance
All egress pathways, fire escapes, electrical switchboards, and fire extinguisher stations must remain clear of debris, tools, and construction materials at all times. Loose materials, electrical cables, and hoses crossing pedestrian walkways must be covered by heavy-duty cable ramps or suspended at a minimum height of 2.4 meters (8 feet). Accumulations of combustible waste (wood shavings, packaging, solvents) must be cleared twice daily: at mid-day and at the close of every shift.

## Section 3: Hot Work Permits and Fire Safety
Any cutting, welding, thermal spraying, or open-flame torch work requires a validated Hot Work Permit issued by the Site Safety Officer at least 2 hours prior to execution. A dedicated Fire Watch individual equipped with a 9 kg ABC dry-powder fire extinguisher must remain stationed at the hot work perimeter during the task and for a mandatory minimum of 60 minutes following the cessation of all hot work activities.
