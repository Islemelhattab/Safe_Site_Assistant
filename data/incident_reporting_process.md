# Incident Notification and Investigation Process

## Section 1: Priority Actions During the First 24 Hours
When a workplace incident, injury, environmental spill, near-miss, or structural damage occurs on site, the following sequence of actions is strictly enforced:
1. Immediate Medical and Rescue Response (0-15 minutes): Provide on-site first aid or summon emergency services (Ambulance / Fire Brigade). Life safety takes precedence over all other protocols.
2. Scene Barricading and Preservation (15-60 minutes): The site supervisor must immediately isolate and seal the incident area using red hazard tape. No tools, equipment, or debris may be altered or moved except to prevent secondary collapse or rescue victims.
3. Verbal Notification (0-2 hours): The supervisor must directly notify the Site Safety Director and the Lead Project Manager via direct telephone call.
4. Initial Incident Flash Report (Within 24 hours): A preliminary one-page Flash Report detailing the date, exact time, individuals involved, immediate root circumstances, and immediate containment measures must be formally submitted to corporate safety executives.

## Section 2: Investigation Timeline and Root Cause Analysis
For incidents categorized as Lost Time Injury (LTI) or High Potential Near Miss (HiPo):
- Formal investigation team convening within 48 hours.
- Detailed Root Cause Analysis (RCA) utilizing the 5-Whys or Fishbone methodology.
- Final Comprehensive Incident Report submission to executive leadership and regulatory authorities within 7 calendar days.
