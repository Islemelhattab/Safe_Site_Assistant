# Scaffolding and Work-at-Height Operating Procedures

## Section 1: Guardrail System Specifications
A standardized perimeter guardrail system is mandatory on all scaffold platforms, edge forms, and elevated walking-working surfaces where the fall risk height is 6 feet (1.8 meters) or higher above a lower level. The maximum allowable platform height before a guardrail is mandatory is 6 feet (1.8 meters).

## Section 2: Structural Integrity and Inspection Criteria
All scaffolding assemblies must be capable of supporting their own weight plus a minimum safety factor of 4 times the maximum intended working load. Supported tubular scaffolds must be anchored to the building structure every 4 meters vertically and every 6 meters horizontally.
Mandatory Inspections:
- Before initial handover for use by the erection team.
- At intervals not exceeding 7 calendar days by a certified competent inspector.
- Immediately following adverse weather events (wind gusts exceeding 65 km/h, heavy snowfall, seismic movement).
- Scaffold green tag must be visibly displayed at the primary access ladder.

## Section 3: Personal Fall Arrest Systems (PFAS)
When guardrails are not feasible, workers must wear a full-body harness compliant with EN 361 connected via an energy-absorbing lanyard to an approved anchorage point rated for at least 22.2 kN (5,000 lbs) per attached worker. Free fall distance must never exceed 1.8 meters (6 feet).
