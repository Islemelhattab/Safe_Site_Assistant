# Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) Mandatory Policy

## Section 1: Minimum Site Mandatory PPE (Level 1)
All individuals present in the operational zone must continuously wear:
1. Head Protection: Industrial safety helmet compliant with EN 397 or ANSI Z89.1 Type 1, Class E. Chin straps are mandatory when working within 2 meters of slab edges.
2. Foot Protection: Steel-toed or composite safety boots compliant with ASTM F2413 or EN ISO 20345 (S3 rating), offering puncture-resistant soles and ankle support.
3. High-Visibility Clothing: Fluorescent reflective vest or jacket meeting ISO 20471 Class 2 minimum during daylight, and Class 3 during night operations.
4. Eye Protection: Safety spectacles with side shields meeting ANSI Z87.1 or EN 166.

## Section 2: Task-Specific Specialized PPE (Level 2)
1. Concrete Cutting, Grinding, and Demolition:
   - Full-face impact shield paired over safety goggles.
   - Respiratory Protection: Half-mask respirator with P100 / FFP3 particulate filters to eliminate crystalline silica dust exposure.
   - Hearing Protection: Earmuffs or banded earplugs rated with a Noise Reduction Rating (NRR) of at least 25 dB.
   - Heavy-duty nitrile or anti-vibration leather gloves.
2. Chemical Handling and Solvent Application:
   - Chemical-resistant butyl or neoprene gauntlet gloves.
   - Vapor cartridge respirators (A2P3 standard) for organic solvent vapors.
   - Chemical splash apron and non-vented protective goggles.
