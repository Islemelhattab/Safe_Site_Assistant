# Heavy Equipment Operation and Maintenance Manual

## Section 1: Daily Pre-Operational Inspection (Mobile Cranes & Excavators)
Operators of mobile hydraulic cranes and crawler excavators must complete and sign a daily pre-operation checklist before starting the engine at the beginning of each shift:
1. Fluid Verification: Check hydraulic oil, engine coolant, transmission fluid, and engine oil levels.
2. Structural Examination: Visually inspect boom pins, slew ring bolts, track tension, and hydraulic cylinders for signs of leakage, stress cracks, or deformation.
3. Safety Devices Check: Verify proper operation of the Load Moment Indicator (LMI), anti-two-block (A2B) limit switches, reverse cameras, and acoustic travel alarms.
If any safety device is defective, the machine must be locked out immediately with a "DO NOT OPERATE" tag.

## Section 2: Exclusion Zones and Slew Radii
A physical barricade must be erected around the entire 360-degree slew radius of the crane or excavator counterweight. No personnel may enter the exclusion zone (minimum 1.5 meters clearance beyond the counterweight swing path) without explicit visual confirmation and two-way radio clearance from the operator.

## Section 3: Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) for Equipment Servicing
Prior to conducting maintenance, oil changes, or mechanical repairs:
- Lower all attachments (buckets, blades, forks) flat to the ground.
- Disengage hydraulic pressure and turn the master ignition key to OFF.
- Place the master battery isolator switch in the open position and attach an OSHA-compliant personal padlock and personalized warning tag.
- Dissipate residual pressure by cycling the control levers three times in all directions.
